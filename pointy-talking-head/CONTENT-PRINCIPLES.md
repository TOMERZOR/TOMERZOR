# Content Principles: Pointy

## How to use this document
These principles guide every Pointy piece that talks to people: talking-head videos, Reels, carousels, captions and
DMs. When unsure, consult this document. When principles conflict, see the prioritization rules below.

**What they were built from.** An audit of the existing Pointy content (the motion ad, the Instagram carousel, the
live-action brief, the "Max" ad and their captions). What worked there: a concrete pain ("14 דקות מדריך בשביל כפתור
אחד"), "על מה לוחצים פה?!", "בדיוק כמו שהייתם שואלים חבר", three simple steps. What to fix: two different calls to
action (DM "פלאגין" vs. "link in bio"), switching between "לך" and "לכם", and "⌥ Space" said as if it were true on
Windows too.

**The facts we may state** (everything else needs a source before it goes on camera): press ⌥ Space in any app · ask
in Hebrew, typed or spoken · an orange cursor points at exactly what to click · step by step, with "עשיתי" to move on ·
works on top of any program · Mac and Windows · מתחילים בחינם.

## Principles

### 1. Start from the moment, not the product
**Statement:** Every piece opens on a moment the viewer lived this week, in a named program, before Pointy is mentioned.

**What this means:**
- The first line names a specific task and a specific program ("זום-אין איטי בפרמייר", "טבלת ציר באקסל"), never "software is hard"
- Pointy's name appears only after the pain has landed (not in the first 3 seconds)
- One moment per piece; we don't list five pains

**Decision test:** Could a viewer say "that happened to me on Tuesday" before they know what we sell?

**Example application:**
> Situation: Choosing the opening line of a talking-head video
> Decision: "14 דקות של מדריך ביוטיוב. בשביל כפתור אחד." not "הכירו את Pointy, עוזר AI חדשני למחשב". The first is a moment; the second is an ad.

---

### 2. Show it, don't claim it
**Statement:** Every claim about what Pointy does is proven on screen in the same breath, in a real recording.

**What this means:**
- When the presenter says "הוא מראה לכם על מה ללחוץ", the screen shows the orange cursor doing it, at real speed
- No adjectives standing in for proof ("מטורף", "גאוני", "מהפכני")
- Times and counts on camera come from the recording itself (a visible timer), never from memory

**Decision test:** If we muted the audio, would the screen still prove the sentence?

**Example application:**
> Situation: The script wanted the line "זה חוסך המון זמן"
> Decision: Replaced with a split screen: a tutorial at 00:18 / 14:37 on one side, the task done with Pointy on the other, and a real stopwatch.

---

### 3. Talk like a friend at the next desk
**Statement:** Spoken, everyday Hebrew, the way you'd explain it to a friend, addressed to "אתם".

**What this means:**
- Plural "אתם/לכם" everywhere: gender-neutral and consistent (no more "לך" in one place and "לכם" in another)
- No product jargon: "AI" at most once, never "בינה מלאכותית מתקדמת", "פתרון", "חוויית משתמש"
- Keys and buttons are named the way people say them: "אופשן ורווח", "עשיתי"

**Decision test:** Would you say this sentence out loud, word for word, to a friend sitting next to you?

**Example application:**
> Situation: Describing the ask bar
> Decision: "שואלים בעברית, כמו שהייתם שואלים חבר" not "ממשק שאילתות בשפה טבעית בעברית"

---

### 4. Only what's true and checkable
**Statement:** We say only what the product does today, exactly as it does it.

**What this means:**
- Stick to the facts list above; anything new (a statistic, a price, an app name, a Windows shortcut) needs a source first
- No invented testimonials, user counts, "saves you X hours" or fake urgency ("רק היום")
- "מתחילים בחינם", never "חינם לגמרי" or "חינם לתמיד"
- A program is named on camera only if it was recorded working in it

**Decision test:** If a viewer tried exactly what we said tonight, would it happen exactly like that?

**Example application:**
> Situation: The draft said "לוחצים אופשן ורווח, במק ובווינדוס"
> Decision: Split it: "אופשן ורווח" over the Mac recording, "במק ובווינדוס" as a separate claim. ⌥ is a Mac key, and the Windows shortcut isn't confirmed yet.

---

### 5. No one is stupid here
**Statement:** The pain is the software's fault, never the viewer's.

**What this means:**
- Blame hidden menus and endless tutorials, not people ("התוכנות לא אומרות לכם איפה הדברים")
- No mocking an age group, "boomers", "אפילו סבתא שלך"
- Relief, not shame: the viewer should feel understood, then capable

**Decision test:** Would someone who struggles with computers feel smarter or smaller after watching?

**Example application:**
> Situation: A hook idea: "עדיין לא יודעים להשתמש באקסל? 😂"
> Decision: Rejected for "אקסל לא אומר לכם איפה הכפתור. Pointy כן."

---

### 6. One ask, made effortless
**Statement:** Every piece ends with one action: "שלחו לי בדירקט את המילה פלאגין".

**What this means:**
- One CTA everywhere: the DM keyword (it replaces "link in bio")
- Say it out loud and show it on screen at the same time, with the word "פלאגין" visible
- The ask comes after the proof, never before; say it at most twice

**Decision test:** Could a viewer who watched with the sound off still know the exact word to send?

**Example application:**
> Situation: The video ends on "מורידים בחינם מהקישור בביו, או שולחים לי פלאגין בדירקט"
> Decision: Cut to one ask: "מתחילים בחינם. שלחו לי בדירקט את המילה פלאגין", with the word in an orange pill on screen.

## Prioritization rules

When principles conflict, use this hierarchy:

1. **Only what's true and checkable** always wins: no line goes on camera that the product doesn't do exactly as said
2. **Show it, don't claim it** takes precedence over pacing: if there's no time to show a claim, cut the claim, not the proof
3. **No one is stupid here** overrides a sharper hook
4. Default to **Start from the moment** when stakes are unclear

### Common conflicts and resolutions

| Conflict | Resolution |
|----------|------------|
| Start from the moment vs Only what's true | The moment must be one Pointy really solves and that was recorded. A punchier pain it hasn't been shown solving waits |
| Talk like a friend vs Only what's true | Casual wording is fine ("כמו קסם" is not: it's a claim). Keep the tone, replace the claim with what happens on screen |
| Show it vs One ask (time) | In a 45-second video the demo gets the time; the CTA is said once at the end, shown on screen and repeated in the caption |
| No one is stupid vs Start from the moment | Describe the moment from the software's side ("התפריט מסתיר את זה") instead of the viewer's ("לא הצלחת") |

## Principles in action

### Applying to a talking-head video
Hook on a moment (1) in the presenter's own words (3) → blame the software (5) → turn to the screen and do it for real
(2) → name only the apps that were recorded (4) → one spoken and on-screen ask (6). 30–45 seconds.

### Applying to captions and DMs
The caption repeats the moment and the one keyword, not a feature list. The DM reply is short and friendly
("הנה הלינק 🎁 תגידו לי איך הלך") and makes no new claims.

---

## Anti-patterns
These violate our principles:
- Opening on the logo or "הכירו את Pointy" (breaks 1)
- "חוסך לכם שעות", "הכלי הכי טוב", "AI מטורף" with nothing on screen to back it (breaks 2 and 4)
- Mixing "לך" and "לכם" in one piece (breaks 3)
- Two CTAs (link in bio + DM) or a CTA before the demo (breaks 6)
- A staged or sped-up screen recording passed off as real time (breaks 2 and 4)
- Jokes at the expense of people who "don't get computers" (breaks 5)
- Saying "אופשן ורווח" as if it were the Windows shortcut (breaks 4)

# Pointy Content Principles — Quick Reference

| Principle | Decision test |
|-----------|---------------|
| Start from the moment | Could they say "that happened to me on Tuesday" before knowing what we sell? |
