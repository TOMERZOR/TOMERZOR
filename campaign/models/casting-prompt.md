# Dream Studios — AI Model Casting Prompt

A prompt for Claude (or any image agent) to build the recurring Dream Studios model roster: six AI models we reuse in every campaign, so each one needs a locked, repeatable look.

---

## The prompt

> You are casting director and photographer for **Dream Studios**, a young streetwear label (graphic tees, logo tees, "Created By God" denim). The brand voice is ironic, warm, a little chaotic, and comes from a group of friends. Think Gen-Z Mediterranean streetwear: Ibiza summers, New York rooftops, overthinking things at 2am.
>
> Create **6 recurring AI models**. Each one should feel like a real person you'd follow on Instagram, not like a stock "AI model". Rules:
>
> 1. **They must be real people, not mannequins.** Give each one visible skin texture, pores, asymmetry, flyaway hair and one unusual feature people will remember. No airbrushed skin, no "AI face" symmetry.
> 2. **Mix them.** Use different ethnicities, face shapes, hair textures, heights and energies. Required: one girl with dense natural freckles and one model with deep dark skin.
> 3. **Make them young and edgy.** Ages 19–25. Each model gets a distinctive style signature (hair, piercings, eyewear, jewellery) that stays the same in every campaign.
> 4. **Don't copy the references.** The Nude Project references set the *mood*: tinted vintage sunglasses, oversized washed layers, deadpan expressions, ironic captions and film-photo grain. Do not reproduce their faces, poses or garments.
> 5. **Shoot them in a studio.** Use a seamless warm off-white paper backdrop, one large soft key light from camera-left and a hard on-camera flash pop for that late-90s editorial snap. Shoot on 35mm-film colour (Portra 400 feel) with fine grain and slightly lifted blacks. The models should look straight into the lens with deadpan, unbothered confidence, or give a small smirk.
> 6. **Dress them in a Dream Studios wardrobe.** Use faded navy, washed black, bone white, tobacco brown and one accent colour (cherry red). Clothes should look boxy and oversized, washed, and lived-in. A small embroidered "dream studios" wordmark may appear on the chest, but don't print other brand logos.
> 7. **Give each model a character bible**: name, age, background, personality, signature look, wardrobe and a sample caption in the brand voice. Then generate a **3:4 studio hero portrait** for each model. Later, use that portrait as the identity reference for full-body shots, group shots and campaigns.
