Support

We read every message. Write to support@shardgame.app and include your device model and the level number if it's about a level.

Restore purchases

Settings or Store › Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

No sound

On iPhone, check that the ring/silent switch isn't on silent, and that Sound effects is on in the game's Settings.

Delete my data

Your progress lives only on your device: deleting the app removes it. For advertising data, see the Privacy Policy.
