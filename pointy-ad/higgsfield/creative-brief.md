# Pointy: "הקיצור הכי שווה במק שלכם". Live-action ad brief for Higgsfield

**Format:** vertical 9:16, 1080p, about 20 seconds (22s generated, cut down to 19–20 in the edit). Hebrew supers (on-screen text), no voiceover.
**Model:** Higgsfield **Cinema Studio 4.0** (`cinematic_studio_video_4_0`, mode `omni_reference`, 1080p, `generate_audio: true`). It is Higgsfield's cinema-grade model: physical camera and lens behaviour, lighting and colour control, native foley, and it starts from a still we approve first.
**Keyframes:** **Nano Banana Pro** (`nano_banana_pro`, 2k, 9:16). It is the most photoreal still model on the account, and it renders small text (the key legends) correctly.

---

## 1. The idea in one line
Everyone has been stuck in front of a screen asking "where is that button?!". The fix is two keys: **⌥ + Space**. The ad opens on those two keys like a jewellery commercial, a macro shot of a finger about to press, and promises the viewer the best shortcut on their Mac. Then it shows the pain, the press, and the relief.

## 2. Why it won't look like AI slop
1. **We approve a still for every shot before animating.** Video is made only from a frame we picked: right hands, right key legends, real skin.
2. **The screen is never generated with readable UI.** AI garbles interfaces and Hebrew. The laptop screen in every shot is defocused, at an angle, or only a pool of warm light. The real Pointy UI (the orange cursor, the ask bar, the step card) and all Hebrew text are composited in the edit from our own HTML. Those are the same assets as the motion ad, so they are pixel-perfect.
3. **Cinema rules in every prompt:** one light motivation per shot (screen, desk lamp, window), 180° shutter motion blur, real depth of field, fine grain, no beauty retouch, imperfect natural movement, and no Apple logo in frame.
4. **One continuous casting and set**, carried shot to shot through the approved hero still as a reference.

## 3. Cast, set, look
- **Talent:** Noa, 27, Israeli, a junior video editor. Olive skin with visible pores and a few freckles, dark curly hair in a loose bun with flyaways, no makeup look. Oversized faded charcoal hoodie, a thin gold ring on her right index finger. Natural, slightly tired eyes. The hand in the macro shots is hers: short unpolished nails, a faint ink mark on a knuckle.
- **Set:** a small Tel Aviv apartment at 11:40 pm. A wooden desk by a window with blurred city lights and a balcony railing outside. A half-drunk glass of iced coffee with melting ice, a sticky note, a phone face down. A **space-black MacBook Pro 14"**: black keys, white backlight glowing faintly, aluminium deck with fine micro-scratches and a fingerprint smudge near the trackpad.
- **Light:** key light from the laptop screen (cool white, later shifting to warm Pointy orange), a practical brass desk lamp camera-left (2700K tungsten), and cool blue moonlight from the window as back rim. The palette is teal night against Pointy orange.
- **Camera language:** ARRI Alexa 35 look, Zeiss Supreme Primes for coverage, a Laowa 24mm probe macro for the keyboard, T1.5–T2 shallow focus, 24 fps, Kodak 2383 print-film colour, gentle halation on highlights, fine 35mm grain. Movement is always motivated and slow (dolly, slider, probe push), never floaty drone moves. One speed-ramp moment only: the key press.

**The retention mechanics:** a curiosity claim in the first frame ("the best shortcut on your Mac") that is only paid off when you see what the keys do, a pattern break from macro to human drama, the pain everyone recognises at second 4, and a payoff by second 12, before the scroll. ## 5. Sound design (final mix)
- **Native audio from Cinema Studio 4.0** is kept for foley: key clicks, trackpad, breath, room tone.
- **Added in the edit:** a minimal electronic bed (sub pulse plus soft plucks, 100 BPM) that starts on the key press in shot 1, drops out under shot 2, comes back on the press in shot 3, and resolves under the CTA. Also the UI sweeteners (bloom, swish, the step pings, the success chime) and a reversed whoosh into each press. I can synthesize these the same way as the motion ad (no licensing).
- **Mix:** dialogue-free. Foley sits slightly forward, the music ducks under every press. Loudness about −14 LUFS for Reels and TikTok.

### 6b. Shots (Cinema Studio 4.0, omni_reference, `start_image` = the approved keyframe, 9:16, 1080p, audio on)

**Negative guidance (append to every shot):** no warped fingers, no extra fingers, no melting keys, no gibberish on-screen text, no Apple logo, no plastic or airbrushed skin, no over-sharpening, no floaty camera, no morphing objects, no lens-flare spam, no stock-footage look.
