**Full description:**

> Grey canvas. Happy paint buckets. One satisfying splash at a time.
>
> **Paint Parade** is the colorful puzzle where cute paint buckets march around a pixel-art canvas and splash their color on every matching pixel they can see. Pick the right bucket at the right time, watch the picture come to life, and add every masterpiece to your gallery!
>
> **HOW TO PLAY**
> 🎨 Tap a bucket to send it onto the parade belt.
> 💦 It splashes every pixel of its color that it can see.
> 😴 Sleepy bucket? Its color is buried inside — peel the outer layers first!
> 🪣 Paint left over? It waits in one of only 3 spots.
> 👆 Every level has a limited number of taps — solve it in the fewest for 3 stars.
> ⚠️ Fill every spot or run out of taps and the level is lost. Think before you tap!
>
> **WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT**
> ✨ Insanely satisfying — grey pixels burst into color with every splash
> 🧠 Easy to learn, hard to master — 200 hand-tuned levels, HARD and SUPER HARD challenges
> 🖼️ Collect 80 adorable artworks in 8 albums: Sweet Treats, Cute Critters, Secret Garden, Sky & Sea, Toy Box, Wonders, Snack Bar and Holidays
> 📅 Daily Canvas — a new painting every day with streak rewards
> 🧲 Fun boosters: +Spot, Grabber and the magical Rainbow bucket
> 🔥 Hot Brush win streaks for bonus spots
> ✈️ Play offline, anytime — no Wi-Fi needed
> 🎵 Relaxing music, juicy sounds and haptics
>
> Perfect for a 2-minute break or a cozy evening session. Download Paint Parade and start your parade of color!
>
> Paint Parade is free to play and contains optional in-app purchases and ads. You can remove forced ads with a one-time purchase.
