Questions designers ask first

Does Lading email my clients?
No. It writes the draft. You read it, change it if you want, and send it yourself.
Do I have to give it my inbox?
No. Most studios already have an orders@ address that vendors write to. You copy that address to Lading, or forward single emails.
What if it reads something wrong?
Every change links to the line it came from, and you can undo it. Vague dates, missing PO numbers and orders that match more than one item always wait for your decision.
Will it replace Studio Designer, Programa or Houzz Pro?
No. Lading is built to sit next to the tool you already use. Keeping it in sync with those tools is the first thing the pilot will test.
What does it cost?
The pilot is free. In return we ask for a short call every two weeks about what worked and what didn't.
