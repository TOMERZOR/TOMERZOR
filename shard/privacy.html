Privacy Policy

SHARD: Satisfying Glass Smash · Last updated October 22, 2026

SHARD is a single-player game. You can play without an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other contact details. This policy explains the little data the game does handle, and who handles it.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (stars, coins, balls you unlocked, settings and your daily streak) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your IP address, device model, operating system, ad interactions and, only if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android).

  • On iOS you are asked through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown.
  • Rewarded videos are always optional: you choose when to watch one.

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

In-app purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them on a new device. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Analytics

This version of SHARD does not use third-party analytics or crash reporting, and we do not sell personal data.

Children

SHARD is a general-audience game that is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn off ad personalization in your device settings (iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking; Android: Settings › Google › Ads).
  • Buy "Remove Ads" to stop interstitial ads.
  • Reset your progress at any time in the game's Settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@shardgame.app
