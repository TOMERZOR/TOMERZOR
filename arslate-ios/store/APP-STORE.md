## 3. App Privacy

**Privacy Policy URL**: `https://tomerzor.github.io/TOMERZOR/arslate/privacy.html`

Get Started → **Yes, we collect data**. מסמנים:

| סוג מידע | למה | מקושר לזהות? | Tracking? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| User Content → **Other User Content** (הטקסט לתרגום) | App Functionality | No | No |
| Identifiers → **Device ID** (מזהה הפרסום, דרך AdMob) | Third-Party Advertising | No | **Yes** |
| Usage Data → **Advertising Data** | Third-Party Advertising | No | **Yes** |
| Usage Data → **Product Interaction** | Third-Party Advertising | No | **Yes** |
| Location → **Coarse Location** (מכתובת ה-IP, אצל Google) | Third-Party Advertising | No | **Yes** |
| Diagnostics → **Crash Data**, **Performance Data** | Third-Party Advertising | No | No |

חשוב: האפליקציה שואלת "לאפשר מעקב?" (App Tracking Transparency), ולכן **חייבים** לענות Tracking = Yes על מזהה הפרסום. ככה נדחתה גרסה של השתול כשהתשובה הייתה "אין מעקב".

## 5. App Review Information (בגרסה 1.0, למטה)

- Sign-in required: **No**
- Contact: השם, הטלפון והמייל שלך.
- **Notes** (באנגלית, בשביל הבודק):
```
ArsLate is a humor app: it rewrites ordinary Hebrew into Israeli street slang ("ars"). The slang includes profanity on purpose; the app is rated 18+.

- The built-in dictionary engine runs fully on the device and works offline, with no limits.
- "Smart" AI translation is optional. Before the first one, the app asks for consent to send the text to our server and from there to Anthropic's Claude. Declining keeps everything on the device.
- 5 AI translations per day are free. After that, the user may choose to watch a rewarded video ad (Google AdMob) for 10 more, or continue with the on-device dictionary. Ads are never shown otherwise.
- No account, no sign-in, no in-app purchases.

To test the ad flow: send 6 translations with the up-arrow button and accept the AI consent sheet on the first one.
```
