# Sample credits

All sounds in this folder are free to use. Each file was trimmed, normalized and converted to mono WAV.

## Drums

- `drums/*808*.wav`, `drums/k808*.wav`, `drums/s808*.wav`, `drums/bass808*.wav`: Roland TR-808 samples recorded by Michael Fischer (1994), released free with no restrictions. Packaged in [fluid-music/open-drums](https://github.com/fluid-music/open-drums).
- `drums/*707*.wav`: Roland TR-707 samples, released into the public domain. Packaged in [fluid-music/open-drums](https://github.com/fluid-music/open-drums).
- `drums/snareAcou*.wav`, `snareGhost.wav`, `rimshot.wav`, `sidestick.wav`, `hatAcou*.wav`, `openAcou.wav`, `clapAcou.wav`, `darbuka*.wav`, `shaker.wav`: [Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) by Versilian Studios, CC0 1.0.

## Instruments (`keys/`)

From the [Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL), CC0 1.0:

- `steinway_*`: Steinway B grand piano
- `harp_*`: concert harp
- `glockenspiel_*`, `vibraphone_*`
- `psaltery_pluck_*`: plucked psaltery, used as a qanun

Glockenspiel, vibraphone and psaltery files sound one octave above the note in their name; `BG.INSTRUMENTS` in `js/engine.js` accounts for that.
