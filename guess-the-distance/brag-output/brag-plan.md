# Guess The Distance: brag video (22.5 s, 1920×1080, Hebrew)

**The angle:** everyone in Israel has an opinion on how long Tel Aviv to Eilat takes. The video opens on that argument, makes you commit to a number on the game's own iOS wheel, then shows the real answer drawn on the map. It's the game's whole loop in the first six seconds, so the viewer has already played one round by the time the logo appears.

**Tone:** default (playful, clean), in the app's own language: system-type Hebrew, glass cards, speed-streaked gradients, springs. Humor comes from the guess being confidently wrong.

**Planning rubric**
1. What is it? A web game: guess real travel times (or road distance) between places, GeoGuessr-style scoring up to 5,000 a round.
2. Who is it for? Israelis who argue about drive times; anyone who likes GeoGuessr.
3. The one thing to remember: you guess on a wheel, then the real route draws itself.
4. Real copy used: "כמה זמן זה לוקח?", "נחשו זמני נסיעה אמיתיים", mode names, "1 על 1", map names.
5. Real UI shown: phone round with the wheel, the reveal with the route, the home screen, a live duel, the desktop version.
6. Hook: a question every Israeli has an opinion on, with a wheel already spinning.
7. Proof points: real routes from OpenStreetMap; 5 modes; live 1v1 with damage; phone and desktop.
8. Punchline: "אז… כמה זמן זה לוקח?" with the address.
9. Format: landscape, for X / LinkedIn / WhatsApp; still readable on a phone.

## Music
Synthesized: a bright, light electronic groove at 112 BPM in D major (plucks, soft kick, claps, sub bass), the same chords as the game's own music. Filtered and sparse under the hook, full from the logo, a drop under the duel's hit, resolving on the outro chord.

## Music cue guidance
No bundled cue presets are installed with this skill here; cues come from the composition's own SFX list (`window.__sfx`), so every tick lands on its frame. Bars land on scene starts (one bar = 2.14 s).
