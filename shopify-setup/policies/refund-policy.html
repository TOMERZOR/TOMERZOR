Our return policy

You can return or exchange any item within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed and undamaged, with the original tags attached. We don't charge a cancellation fee.

How to return

  1. Email [hello@your-domain.com] with your order number and the items you're returning.
  2. We'll reply within 2 business days with the return address and instructions.
  3. Pack the items securely and send them back. Keep your shipping receipt until your refund arrives.

Return shipping is paid by you, unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item.

Refunds

We refund you to your original payment method within 14 days of receiving your returned items. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. The original delivery fee is refunded only if the item was faulty or wrong.

Exchanges

Need a different size? Tell us in your return email. We'll send the new size as soon as your return arrives, subject to stock. Exchanges ship free.

Faulty or wrong items

If something arrives faulty or isn't what you ordered, email us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or a full refund including delivery costs.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order at no cost before it ships by emailing us. Once it has shipped, follow the return steps above.

Your legal rights

Our policy is in addition to your rights under the Israeli Consumer Protection Law, 1981. For online purchases, the law lets you cancel within 14 days of receiving the item. If you are 65 or older, a person with a disability or a new immigrant (up to 5 years since immigration), and the purchase included a conversation with us (for example by phone), you can cancel within 4 months. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.
