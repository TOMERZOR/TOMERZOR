We want everyone to be able to shop with us. This site is built to meet Israeli Standard 5568, based on WCAG 2.0 level AA, in line with the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013.

What we've done

  • The whole site works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Images have text descriptions for screen readers.
  • Buttons, forms and menus have clear labels, and headings follow a logical order.
  • Text and background colours have strong contrast.
  • The layout adapts to phones, tablets and computers, and text can be enlarged up to 200%.
  • Animations switch off when your device's “reduce motion” setting is on.

Known limitations

We keep reviewing the site. If you find a page or feature that's hard to use, please tell us and we'll fix it or help you complete your purchase another way.
