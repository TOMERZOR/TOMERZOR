# Pointy — "the cursor is the film" · 15 s · 1080×1920 · Hebrew

Concept: one orange point carries the whole take. It is born from the ⌥ Space press, becomes the caret that types
the question, tips over into the Pointy arrow, points at the exact button in three different apps, folds the world
back into itself and finally becomes the arrow inside the Pointy icon. No cards replace each other, no cuts.

Look: warm paper (`#F4EFE7`), ink line UI, Heebo. Pointy orange is the only colour on screen, so the eye always
lands on the point. A faint dot lattice behind everything gives the camera something to move against.
