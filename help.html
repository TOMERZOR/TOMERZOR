Shipping

Where we ship

We currently ship within Israel only. International shipping is coming soon.

Processing time

Orders are packed and handed to the courier within of payment. Orders placed on Friday, Saturday or a public holiday are processed on the next business day.

Delivery times start once your order has shipped and are counted in business days (Sunday–Thursday). You'll see the exact cost and expected delivery date at checkout before you pay.

Tracking

When your order ships, we email you a tracking number and a link to follow your parcel.

Delivery problems

  • Late parcel: if your order hasn't arrived 3 business days after the latest expected date, contact us and we'll chase it with the courier.
  • Lost or damaged parcel: we're responsible for your order until it's delivered. If it's lost or arrives damaged, we'll send a replacement or a full refund.
  • Wrong address: check your address at checkout. If a parcel comes back to us because of an incorrect address, we'll contact you to resend it. The second delivery may be charged.

Returns & Refunds

Last updated

Our return policy

You can return or exchange any item within days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed and undamaged, with the original tags attached. We don't charge a cancellation fee.

How to return

  1. Email with your order number and the items you're returning.
  2. We'll reply within 2 business days with the return address and instructions.
  3. Pack the items securely and send them back. Keep your shipping receipt until your refund arrives.

Return shipping is paid by you, unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item.

Refunds

We refund you to your original payment method within days of receiving your returned items. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. The original delivery fee is refunded only if the item was faulty or wrong.

Exchanges

Need a different size? Tell us in your return email. We'll send the new size as soon as your return arrives, subject to stock. Exchanges ship free.

Faulty or wrong items

If something arrives faulty or isn't what you ordered, email us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement or a full refund including delivery costs.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order at no cost before it ships by emailing us. Once it has shipped, follow the return steps above.

Your legal rights

Our policy is in addition to your rights under the Israeli Consumer Protection Law, 1981. For online purchases, the law lets you cancel within 14 days of receiving the item. If you are 65 or older, a person with a disability or a new immigrant (up to 5 years since immigration), and the purchase included a conversation with us (for example by phone), you can cancel within 4 months. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

Privacy Policy

Last updated

This policy explains what personal information (“Dream Studios”, “we”) collects when you use this website or buy from us, why we collect it, and what rights you have. It is written to meet the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 1981, and, for visitors in the European Union, the GDPR.

Who is responsible for your data

, , . Contact us about privacy at .

What we collect

  • Orders: your name, delivery address, phone number, email address and what you bought.
  • Payments: handled by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Newsletter: your email address, year of birth and the date you gave consent.
  • Customer service: the messages you send us.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser and device type, kept in our hosting provider's server logs for security.
  • On your device: your bag, wishlist and cookie choices, stored in your browser. See the Cookie Policy.

You don't have to give us your information, but we can't process an order without your contact and delivery details.

Why we use it

  • To process, deliver and support your orders, including returns (needed to fulfil our contract with you).
  • To keep tax and accounting records, as required by law.
  • To send marketing emails, only if you've agreed. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent fraud.

Who we share it with

We share only what's needed with service providers who work for us: website hosting, payment processing, delivery couriers and our email service. They may only use your data to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information when the law requires it. We never sell your personal information.

Some of these providers store data outside Israel. When that happens, we use providers that protect your data to a standard at least equal to Israeli law and, for EU visitors, the GDPR.

How long we keep it

  • Order and invoice records: 7 years, as required by Israeli tax law.
  • Newsletter details: until you unsubscribe.
  • Customer service messages: 2 years after the conversation ends.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it where we don't need to keep it by law. You can withdraw your marketing consent at any time with the unsubscribe link in any email. If you're in the EU, you can also ask us to restrict or object to processing and to receive your data in a portable format.

Email and we'll answer within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority or, in the EU, to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our newsletter is only for people aged or older, and we don't knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us their details, contact us and we'll delete them.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, access to customer data is limited to people who need it, and payments go through a certified payment provider.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let customers know by email.

Accessibility Statement

Last updated

We want everyone to be able to shop with us. This site is built to meet Israeli Standard 5568, based on WCAG 2.0 level AA, in line with the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013.

What we've done

  • The whole site works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Images have text descriptions for screen readers.
  • Buttons, forms and menus have clear labels, and headings follow a logical order.
  • Text and background colours have strong contrast.
  • The layout adapts to phones, tablets and computers, and text can be enlarged up to 200%.
  • Animations switch off when your device's “reduce motion” setting is on.

Known limitations

We keep reviewing the site. If you find a page or feature that's hard to use, please tell us and we'll fix it or help you complete your purchase another way.
