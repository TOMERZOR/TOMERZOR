# Market research: mobile games, October 2026

Most figures come from aggregators (Sensor Tower and AppMagic as cited by blogs). Treat them as directional.

## Where the money is
- **Highest IAP revenue:** Strategy (~$9.3B, H1 2026), Puzzle (~$8.1B), RPG (~$6.1B). These are big-studio genres with expensive UA (Last War, Whiteout Survival, Honor of Kings).
- **Most downloaded:** Puzzle.
- **Fastest growing:** hybrid-casual. IAP revenue up about 23% in H1 2026, with the top 10 titles growing 67–100%. ARPDAU is $0.15–0.50, against $0.03–0.08 for hypercasual. In puzzle the mix is about 59% IAP and 41% ads.
- **Downloads are falling overall:** down 12–25% in H1 2026, so retention and LTV now matter more than install volume.

## What is climbing the charts now
| Game | Type | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Meowdoku** (Learnings) | Logic grid, one cat per region | #1 Google Play top free (18 Oct). +1,197% installs in June, 10.1M. Relaxing and brainy. **Main complaint: too many ads.** |
| **Arrows – Puzzle Escape** | Minimalist extraction | 100M+ downloads, topped global downloads in May. Simple, no timer. Aggressive monetization. |
| **Bus Fever Party** (Lumi) | Color-match parking jam | #1 download growth in September, 12.8M. |
| **Color Block: Combo Blast** | Block placement | 10.4M installs in September. Block Blast is still the giant (~19M/month). |
| **Smash Fest!** (Flow Games) | Physics smash | Top 3 US iOS with **zero paid UA**, 2.9M in its first month. ASMR audio, precise haptics, near-miss tension. |
| **Screwdom** | Screw-sort | About $200M lifetime, 45M downloads. |

## Conclusions
1. For a solo developer the money is in **hybrid-casual puzzle**: huge download volume, cheap to build, and organic growth through TikTok is possible.
2. The gap in the market is that the leaders **annoy players with ads**. A polished game with fair monetization can win on ratings and retention.
3. The free growth engine is game feel: sound, haptics, satisfaction ("oddly satisfying" works on TikTok), as Smash Fest showed.
4. Retention comes from a daily challenge, streaks, a collection or world to unlock, and endless levels with guaranteed solvability.
