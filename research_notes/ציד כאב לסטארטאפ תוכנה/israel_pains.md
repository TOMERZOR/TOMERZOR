# Israel-specific pains for Israeli SMBs, עצמאים, professionals and households that software could solve (2024–2026)

> Method note for the report writer: every page fetch (WebFetch and curl) was blocked by the session's egress proxy for every host tried (boi.org.il, israelhayom.co.il, ice.co.il, time.com, greeninvoice.co.il). **All findings below come from search-engine result excerpts and summaries, not from full-page reads.** Quotes are as the search tool returned them, often translated or paraphrased. Treat numbers as "reported by the cited page" and check the important ones before relying on them. Many sources are vendor or consultant marketing pages, and they are marked as such.

## Q1. What do Israeli עצמאים and small businesses complain about most, and how big is the macro pain?

### Takeaway
The setting is a war-hit SMB sector: about 61,000 businesses closed in 2025, a 5-year high, and about half of עצמאים earn less than before the war. Layered on top is a steady stream of new compliance work, mainly the חשבוניות ישראל allocation-number threshold falling 25k → 10k → 5k NIS during 2026, the new עוסק זעיר track, and several war-compensation tracks. Long-running structural pains also persist: long supplier-credit terms and costlier credit for small firms, payroll errors, and tax-authority debt and advance-payment friction.

### Cited Findings
**Macro / war context**
- CofaceBDI: about 61,000 companies and businesses closed in Israel in 2025, versus about 37,500 opened (net drop of about 23,400). That is the highest closure count in five years (2024 ≈ 59,000; 2023 ≈ 57,000). Most were small and medium businesses, and CofaceBDI's 2025 business risk index was 6.5, a 3-year high. — [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/business/article/19527208); [ICE](https://www.ice.co.il/research/news/article/1096933)
- CofaceBDI's CEO blamed closures on the war's economic cost, saying many SMBs could not survive falling demand and unstable cash flow. Center/Sharon accounted for 37% of closures. — [ICE](https://www.ice.co.il/research/news/article/1096933)
- One report says closures rose 6% in Q1 2026 and forecasts about 16,200 more failures. This comes from a single source and was not verified. — [INN](https://www.inn.co.il/news/695585)
- Israel Democracy Institute survey (May 2026), as relayed by the search summary: 64% of self-employed respondents reported lower business activity than before 7 Oct 2023, with an average decline of 51%. TIME reports that about half of Israel's self-employed earn less than before the war. — [TIME, Jul 2026](https://time.com/article/2026/07/13/israel-war-economy-cost-ordinary-israelis/)
- TIME describes a kite-surfing instructor who served more than 400 days in reserves and left his business in debt. It reports that reservists' "traditional credit options remain largely inaccessible due to the instability of their earnings." — [TIME](https://time.com/article/2026/07/13/israel-war-economy-cost-ordinary-israelis/); see also [Israel First TV, Oct 2026: "Three years after October 7, Israeli reservists are still fighting to save their businesses"](https://israelfirsttvprogram.substack.com/p/friday-october-16-2026-three-years)
- Bank of Israel small and micro business survey 2025 (published June 2026): 4,029 respondents, and 75% work alone with no employees. Most Israeli SMBs are therefore solo operators. — [BOI PDF](https://www.boi.org.il/media/mtefr3ky/סקר-עסקים-זעירים-וקטנים-2025.pdf)

**Compliance churn: חשבוניות ישראל (allocation numbers)**
- The allocation-number threshold started at 25,000 NIS, fell to 10,000 NIS on 1.1.2026 and to 5,000 NIS before VAT on 1.6.2026. Without an allocation number the *buyer* cannot deduct input VAT. — [Green Invoice/Morning guide](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/magazine/israel-invoice/); [Baker Tilly](https://bakertilly.co.il/blog-invoices-2026); [Stark CPA](https://www.starkcpa.com/)
- A 2025 economic-efficiency law sped up the decline, and the Tax Authority may refuse allocation requests. A 28.4.2025 Tax Authority presentation covers what supplier and customer do on refusal, including a "reverse charge" (היפוך חיוב) option. — [Brit CPA](https://britcpa.co.il/hozrim/%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%93%D7%9C-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C-%D7%A2%D7%93%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9C%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%AA-2026/); [Capitax](https://www.capitax.co.il/content/2/3434)
- SMB representatives and accountants opposed the planned 1.1.2025 expansion. — [ICE](https://www.ice.co.il/law/news/article/1033930)
- "Small businesses that rarely met the requirement before will now encounter it weekly." A business issuing about 10 invoices a month in the 6,000–15,000 NIS range would suddenly need about 10 numbers a month. — [vuz.co.il](https://vuz.co.il/knowledge-center/israel-invoices-allocation-number-guide-2026/); [Lazy Invoice](https://lazyinvoice.co.il/israel-invoice-model-2026-guide/)
- Commonly reported operational problems are connection failures, slow interface responses and invoices delayed in receiving a number. — [hanhach.co.il](https://hanhach.co.il/invoice-israel/)
- About one in eight supplier tax invoices needs an allocation number, and many business customers now verify the number when they receive an invoice (verification runs through the Tax Authority personal area). — [Linet](https://www.linet.org.il/verify-supplier-allocation-number/); [Optipay](https://www.optipay.co.il/blog/allocation-number); [menahel4u (SAP B1)](https://menahel4u.com/support-center/%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A6%D7%93-%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A8-%D7%94%D7%94%D7%A7%D7%A6%D7%90%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%A0%D7%95-%D7%9E%D7%94/)
- A vendor forecasts that allocation numbers will become mandatory on *all* B2B invoices within 2–3 years. This is a forecast, not an official schedule. — [basecrm.co.il](https://basecrm.co.il/guides/allocation-number-invoices-2026/)

**Compliance churn: עוסק זעיר and עוסק פטור**
- The new עוסק זעיר track is an income-tax status only (not VAT). It came into force in 2025 and runs fully in 2026, with a fixed 30% deduction from turnover and no receipts needed. The file is reclassified if the short annual report or tax coordination is not filed. — [lilachbullock.com](https://www.lilachbullock.com/osek-zair/); [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/guides/news/article/20045314)
- Sources disagree on the 2026 ceilings: 122,833 NIS (עוסק פטור, indexed) versus 120,000 NIS (עוסק זעיר). The confusion itself shows the compliance burden. — [msl.org.il](https://msl.org.il/guides/tikrat-mahzor-shnatit/); [tomerdtax](https://tomerdtax.co.il/blog/osek-patur-ceiling-switch-to-murshe)

**Tax authority debt and advances**
- The most common source of debt for self-employed people is the gap between advances (מקדמות) and the final assessment. Where no return is filed, the Authority sets an arbitrary, usually higher, amount, and it can lien or offset without going to court under the Collection Ordinance. — [Morning guide](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/magazine/business-management/income-tax-advances-the-complete-guide/); [law-chovot.co.il](https://www.law-chovot.co.il/authorities/tax-authority); [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%AA%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%99_%D7%9E%D7%A1_%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%94_%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%A2%D7%9C_%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7_%D7%A2%D7%A6%D7%9E%D7%90%D7%99_%D7%A9%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%95_%D7%97%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%94)
- Annual-report deadlines for tax year 2025 were pushed back "due to the security situation" (31.5.2026 for individuals not filing online, 30.6.2026 for online filers, 30.7.2026 for companies). A separate extension arrangement for representatives (מייצגים) uses monthly filing quotas. — [Claltax](https://claltax.com/); [Efraty CPA](https://www.efraty.com/%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%93%D7%A8-%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%93%D7%95%D7%97%D7%95%D7%AA-2025/)

**War-compensation bureaucracy (קרן פיצויים / נזק עקיף / מילואים)**
- Compensation fund totals as reported in Oct 2026: about 38.3B NIS paid in all, including about 1 million claims worth about 30B NIS for business lost profits and losses. An earlier report gave 22.5B NIS and 824k claims. — [hon.co.il](https://www.hon.co.il/%D7%A7%D7%A8%D7%9F-%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D-38-3-%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%93-168-%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%A3-%D7%A0%D7%96%D7%A7-%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8/); [Davar](https://www.davar1.co.il/651306/)
- Mako (2026): businesses hit in "עם כלביא" (June 2025) were still waiting for compensation 8 months later. Many received only partial advances, statuses stayed "בטיפול" for a long time, and the proof required was hard to meet. — [Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/news-business/news/Article-6c23540d40a1d91026.htm)
- "עם כלביא" indirect-damage track: turnover had to be 12,000 NIS to 400M NIS (2022), with a decline of at least 25% (monthly filers) or 12.5% (bi-monthly filers). Fixed-expense coefficients run 7–22%, and the wage formula is 75% × 1.25 of qualifying wages. Deadline was 20.10.2025. — [JDN](https://www.jdn.co.il/news/2447625/); [BSH CPA](https://www.bshcpa.co.il/%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A2%D7%9D-%D7%9B%D7%9C%D7%91%D7%99%D7%90/); [KPMG](https://kpmg.co.il/%D7%A2%D7%9D-%D7%9B%D7%9C%D7%91%D7%99%D7%90-%D7%A2%D7%93%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D)
- Another track was drafted for March–April 2026 (red/orange restrictions lasting more than 45 cumulative days), with a temporary-provision regulation draft and a Knesset Finance Committee bill in April 2026. — [tazkirim.gov.il](https://www.tazkirim.gov.il/s/legislativeworkactivity/a13Wn00000BN5wkIAD/%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A6%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8?language=en_US); [Knesset](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/Committees/25_cs_sl_12746955.pdf)
- The State Comptroller published a January 2026 report on the Tax Authority's emergency preparedness and activity, which covers indirect compensation. — [Mevaker PDF](https://library.mevaker.gov.il/sites/DigitalLibrary/Documents/2026/Emergency/2026-Emergency-101-Tax.pdf)
- Reservist (צו 8) indirect-damage grant: separate windows for May–June 2025 (deadline 31.12.2025) and Sep–Oct 2025 (deadline 31.3.2026). Eligibility was more than 30 consecutive days, later relaxed to more than 21. 1.5B NIS was added to the support fund in 2025, and the grant is run jointly by the IDF support fund and the Tax Authority's compensation fund. — [ICE](https://www.ice.co.il/research/news/article/1049219); [Funder](https://www.funder.co.il/article/191351); [Amir CPA](https://www.amir-cpa.net/post/%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A0%D7%A7%D7%99-%D7%A0%D7%96%D7%A7-%D7%A2%D7%A7%99%D7%A3-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A9%D7%9C-%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%AA-2026-%D7%9B%D7%9C-%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%A9%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%91-%D7%9C%D7%93%D7%A2%D7%AA)
- Employers of reservists claim benefits back from ביטוח לאומי through an online system for employers with up to 10 reservist employees (claims before 1.10.2023 use paper form 501). A separate 20% social-contributions compensation covers 7.10.2023–31.12.2025, and the benefit calculation changed from 1.5.2025. Nothing found on reimbursement *delays*. — [BTL](https://www.btl.gov.il/HaravotBarzel1/KizbeotHB/MiloeimHB/Pages/MasikimMiloeim.aspx); [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%99_%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%A9%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%95_%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8_%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%91%D7%93%D7%99%D7%94%D7%9D_%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A4%D7%AA_%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%97%D7%9E%D7%AA_%D7%97%D7%A8%D7%91%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%96%D7%9C)

**Getting paid (שוטף פלוס, collections)**
- Average supplier-credit days were 72 in 2015 (SME Agency, Ministry of Economy). A BDI figure put them at 96 days in 2011, "more than double the UK, triple France", peaking at 102. The small business "becomes the bank of the large body", and small firms paid about 6% interest versus about 3% for large ones. **No figure after 2015 was found.** — [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/article/569807); [TheMarker 2012](https://www.themarker.com/news/2012-04-18/ty-article/0000017f-e1f2-d7b2-a77f-e3f739570000)
- The 2017 חוק מוסר תשלומים ("שוטף+30/45") excludes HMOs, hospitals and MDA, and contractors get שוטף+70/80 from public bodies. — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local/articles/0,7340,L-3679788,00.html); [Davar](https://www.davar1.co.il/56567/)
- Collection lawyers typically charge 15–25% of the amount collected or 500–1,500 NIS/hour. Returned checks and promissory notes go straight to הוצאה לפועל (a short track applies under 25,000 NIS). A liquidated-sum claim covers up to 75,000 NIS and takes 60–90 days without opposition, or months to a year with it. — [law-chovot.co.il](https://www.law-chovot.co.il/blog/chovot-esek-katan-eich-yotzim-2026); [gatrimon-law](https://www.gatrimon-law.co.il/%D7%92%D7%91%D7%99%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%91%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%97%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%9D/)
- Checks are still big: in 2021 check turnover was 812B NIS against 382B NIS in debit cards, and more than 120B NIS went through check discounting, which finances small businesses. The Bank of Israel ran a consultation on a *digital replacement for the paper check*. — [BOI press release](https://boi.org.il/publications/pressreleases/הארכת-תקופת-קול-הקורא-להתייעצות-בנושא-אפיון-טכנולוגי-לתחליף-דיגיטלי-לצק-מנייר/)

**Payroll**
- An older survey of about 2,500 payslips (חברת עוקץ) found an error rate of 16.3% at small businesses versus 10.7% at medium ones. Common errors are overtime (125%/150%), missed minimum-wage updates and pension contribution base. **The survey is from 2017–18.** — [Walla](https://finance.walla.co.il/item/3043182); [TheMarker 2018](https://www.themarker.com/career/2018-05-28/ty-article/0000017f-ea91-d4cd-af7f-ebf93dbd0000); [bsachar](https://www.bsachar.co.il/%D7%98%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A0%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%AA%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8/)
- Outsourced payslips cost 20–60 NIS per slip, often with a monthly minimum. A one-off payslip audit costs 800–1,500 NIS. — [B144 price list](https://www.b144.co.il/PriceList/%D7%94%D7%A0%D7%94%D7%9C%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA/%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%AA%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8/); [Midrag](https://www.midrag.co.il/Content/Tip/30155)

**Digital and AI adoption gap (an opportunity signal)**
- SME Agency survey (2025): 28% of Israeli businesses use AI as an integral part of the business. Usage is 31% for self-employed, 26% micro, 18% small and 32% medium businesses, and about two-thirds of AI users pay for tools. The Ministry says digital-tool use in commerce, non-tech services and traditional industry "is still lower than desired", and it launched a 9M NIS digitization/AI grant track. — [SBA](https://www.sba.org.il/hb/PolicyAndInformation/Researches/Pages/sr90.aspx); [TheMarker](https://www.themarker.com/career/2025-07-15/ty-article/.premium/00000198-0dfd-d43e-adbc-7dfd22860000); [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/BizTech/news/article/20033280); [IDI PDF](https://www.idi.org.il/media/28643/practical-adoption-of-ai-in-business.pdf)

### Inferences
- The most Israel-specific, fastest-changing pains are regulatory events: allocation numbers, עוסק זעיר and repeated war-compensation tracks. Regulatory churn creates recurring demand and makes foreign tools structurally unable to compete. It also gives local incumbents a reason to ship the same feature quickly.
- Most SMBs are solo (75% in the BOI survey), so products should be priced and designed for one-person businesses, or sold through the accountant who serves dozens or hundreds of them.
- War-related pains are large in money terms (tens of billions NIS, about 1M claims) but episodic, and their rules change with each operation.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 figure for Israeli average payment days (DSO) or the returned-check rate was found. BOI and BDI data would be needed.
- No quantified Lahav / chambers-of-commerce survey ranking SMB complaints was found. Searches returned nothing relevant.
- The IDI 64% / 51% figures come via a search summary of TIME and were not read in the original.

## Q2. Which Israeli products exist, at what prices, and what do users complain about?

### Takeaway
Invoicing, payments, appointment booking, shift scheduling, ועד בית, kindergarten management, tender alerts and tax-refund apps are all crowded with local Hebrew-first players at 30–400 NIS/month. The gaps are less "no Hebrew tool exists" and more in workflows *between* systems: verifying supplier invoices, chasing payments, preparing compensation claims, reconciling insurance commissions and contractor progress billing.

### Cited Findings
- **Morning (formerly Green Invoice):** claims 165,000 business owners and says it is the largest invoicing system in Israel. Plans are Basic/Best/Extra from 29 NIS/month with a 30-day free trial. Founded 2012. — [Morning exempt-dealer page](https://www.greeninvoice.co.il/exempt-dealer/); [financa review](https://financa.co.il/חשבונית-ירוקה-ביקורת/)
- **iCount vs Morning:** a Midrag survey of 50 professionals put Green Invoice first (34%) and iCount third (15%). Poptin lists iCount at 59 NIS and Green Invoice at 57.5 NIS for up to 200 documents a month. YYDevelopment lists iCount from 22 NIS for 10 documents. — [Midrag](https://biz.midrag.co.il/Content/Article/14743); [Poptin](https://www.poptin.co.il/blog/%D7%98%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%95%D7%90%D7%94-%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%98/); [YYDevelopment](https://www.yydevelopment.co.il/green-invoice-sites/)
- **Complaints about invoicing tools:** the main recurring complaint is no phone support. Poptin lists phone support as "no" for iCount, Green Invoice and others, and one user on a review page says there is no phone line and their account was deleted (a single unverified account). Forum users on hasolidit are mostly satisfied with Green Invoice at about 30 NIS/month. **No evidence of a recent price-hike backlash was found.** — [Poptin](https://www.poptin.co.il/blog/%D7%98%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%95%D7%90%D7%94-%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%98/); [freepatentsbank review](https://freepatentsbank.com/bikorot/%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA/icount); [hasolidit thread](https://www.hasolidit.com/kehila/threads/%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%A4%D7%A9-%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%A7-%D7%9C%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%92%D7%99%D7%98%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%AA.13584/)

## Q3. Professional niches with heavy manual work

### Takeaway
The niches with clear evidence of manual, Israel-specific workflows are accountants and bookkeepers (allocation numbers, compensation claims, filing quotas), insurance and pension agents (commission reconciliation, מסלקה data) and renovation contractors (quotes → progress bills → collection). ועד בית and kindergartens are already served.

### Cited Findings
- **Accountants:** a vendor-style article claims AI "is set to replace up to 60% of routine audit tasks" and that automating 10 hours a week saves about 46,800 NIS a year. The surveys behind the 60% are unnamed, so treat it as a weak claim. — [businessai.co.il](https://businessai.co.il/en/news/israels-accounting-firms-face-digital-overhaul-by-2026)
- Accountants face monthly filing-quota extension arrangements, and only reports transmitted via the full online system count toward the quota. — [Claltax](https://claltax.com/)
- Compensation claims require CPA-heavy calculations (fixed-expense coefficients, wage formulas, turnover-decline tests), and many CPA firms publish guides on them. That makes them a natural service for representatives (מייצגים). — [BSH CPA](https://www.bshcpa.co.il/%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A2%D7%9D-%D7%9B%D7%9C%D7%91%D7%99%D7%90/); [Frolinger](https://www.frolinger.co.il/%D7%A4%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%97%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%AA-%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A2%D7%9D-%D7%9B%D7%9C%D7%91%D7%99%D7%90/)
- **Insurance agents:** commissions paid by insurers to agents totalled 10.9B NIS in 2023 (another source says 13.2B NIS a year) and rose 46% in five years. Commission types are היקף (on premium/deposit) and נפרעים (an annual % of accumulated savings). — [Bizportal 13.2B](https://www.bizportal.co.il/Insurance/news/article/20045914); [Bizportal +46%](https://www.bizportal.co.il/capitalmarket/news/article/20041548)
- Common commission errors are missing commissions, wrong rates and unmanaged clawbacks. The advice is to collect commission agreements and reports, reconcile them against the client list and use automated anomaly detection. — [commission.co.il](https://commission.co.il/guides.html)
- Regulatory risk: a Treasury/regulator team recommended that clients pay agents directly rather than through insurers, at a uniform fee. It is not yet law. — [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/bjumpzqvke); [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/Insurance/news/article/20012575)
- The מסלקה פנסיונית: 2013 reports said it "מקרטעת" for agents. In 2026, CRMs such as שווה "פיננסי 360" pull production from it. — [Calcalist 2013](https://www.calcalist.co.il/articles/0,7340,L-3610942,00.html); [Funder 2026](https://www.funder.co.il/article/197190)
- **Contractors:** public bodies may pay contractors שוטף+70 and local authorities שוטף+80, which makes receivables and cash flow especially painful. — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local/articles/0,7340,L-3679788,00.html)
- **Business licensing (רישוי עסקים):** reforms are under way (amendment 34 differential licensing, amendment 39 exempting some summer camps from 2.2.2025, Health Ministry food-business reform), and the government planned full licensing digitization by end of 2025. — [Kfar Saba](https://www.kfar-saba.muni.il/%D7%94%D7%A8%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%94%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%D7%94-%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D/); [lawipedia](https://lawipedia.co.il/2025/08/18/%D7%94%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%99-%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%A2%D7%A1%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C-2025-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9A-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A7/); [techloft](https://techloft.co.il/licensing-reform-2025/)
- **ארנונה (businesses):** a district court ruled that collecting under an unauthorized ארנונה order creates a restitution claim even when the השגה/ערר deadlines have long passed. That case concerned about 290k NIS paid in 2017–2023 to Mateh Binyamin. Appeals committees publish monthly decisions (e.g., Tel Aviv, April 2025). — [Nadlan Center](https://www.nadlancenter.co.il/article/15343); [Tel Aviv ערר decisions](https://www.tel-aviv.gov.il/Transparency/DocLib3/החלטות ועדת ערר לענייני ארנונה - אפריל 2025.pdf)

### Inferences
- Insurance agents are a high-ARPU, data-heavy, Israel-specific niche, since the מסלקה, היקף/נפרעים commissions and Israeli insurers are all local. The pending regulatory change to fee flows is both a risk and a trigger for new software.
- Accountants are the best *distribution channel* for anything SMB-compliance-related: one CPA office serves many solo businesses.

### Gaps
- Lawyers (נט המשפט workflows), clinics, real-estate brokers, תמ"א/פינוי בינוי and moving were not researched (budget). No evidence was collected for them.
- No count of insurance agents or accounting offices in Israel was found.

## Q6. Ranked pain list with SURVIVES / DROPPED verdicts and devil's advocate

### Takeaway
6 pains SURVIVE (two conditionally) and 9 are DROPPED, mostly because of crowded local incumbents or free government services. The strongest are: (1) buyer-side supplier-invoice intake with allocation-number verification, sold via bookkeepers; (2) automated Hebrew B2B collections (WhatsApp → formal warning → הוצל"פ handoff); (3) a war-compensation and reservist-grant claim workbench for CPAs; (4) commission reconciliation for insurance agents.

### Cited Findings (each item cites evidence above. Verdicts are the researcher's judgment)

**1. Supplier-invoice intake + allocation-number verification (buyer side): SURVIVES (strong, time-sensitive)**
- Pain: from 1.6.2026 any B2B tax invoice above 5,000 NIS pre-VAT without a valid allocation number loses its input-VAT deduction. About one in eight supplier invoices is affected, and the threshold is expected to fall further. — [Stark CPA](https://www.starkcpa.com/); [Linet](https://www.linet.org.il/verify-supplier-allocation-number/); [basecrm](https://basecrm.co.il/guides/allocation-number-invoices-2026/)
- Who suffers: every עוסק מורשה buying B2B, and bookkeepers who type in supplier invoices. Cost: 18% VAT lost per non-compliant invoice above the threshold.
- Existing: Linet, SAP B1 partner guides, Optipay (AP). Issuer-side tools (Morning, iCount, Invoice4u, Hyp) focus on *getting* numbers.
- Gap: a lightweight AI inbox (email/WhatsApp/photo) that extracts supplier invoices, checks allocation numbers automatically, flags missing or invalid ones and chases the supplier for a corrected invoice, with export to Hashavshevet/Rivhit/Priority.
- Devil's advocate: accounting systems (Hashavshevet, Rivhit, Priority) and Morning's expense module may add verification. The Tax Authority may make verification trivial or automatic. Global extension is via the e-invoicing/clearance trend (EU ViDA, Poland KSeF, India), but each country differs.

**2. Getting paid: B2B collections, שוטף+ terms, returned checks: SURVIVES**
- Pain: historical supplier credit of 72 days (2015) to 96 days (2011), with small firms paying about 6% versus about 3% interest. Collection lawyers take 15–25%. There were about 61k closures in 2025, linked to cash flow. — [Israel Hayom](https://www.israelhayom.co.il/article/569807); [law-chovot](https://www.law-chovot.co.il/blog/chovot-esek-katan-eich-yotzim-2026); [ICE](https://www.ice.co.il/research/news/article/1096933)
- Existing: invoicing tools send payment links and some reminders (not verified per vendor), collection lawyers and agencies, check-discounting firms.
- Gap: an automated Hebrew dunning ladder over WhatsApp, SMS and email that escalates to a legally formatted מכתב התראה, then a generated הוצל"פ / תביעה בסדר דין מקוצר package, tied to the invoicing system.
- Devil's advocate: Morning or Grow could add dunning, and many SMBs fear harming relationships. Globally there are Chaser and Upflow (UK/EU/US), so global extension means competing with them. No post-2015 Israeli DSO data was found, so the pain level is partly inferred.

**3. War-compensation and reservist-grant claim workbench (for CPAs and SMBs): SURVIVES (conditional, episodic)**
- Pain: about 1M indirect-damage claims and about 30B NIS. Multiple tracks (Swords of Iron, עם כלביא June 2025, March–April 2026, reservist grants with windows and deadlines), each with formulas, coefficients and proofs. Mako reports businesses still waiting 8 months on, with "בטיפול" statuses and hard-to-meet proof requirements. — [hon.co.il](https://www.hon.co.il/%D7%A7%D7%A8%D7%9F-%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%99%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%99%D7%99%D7%9D-38-3-%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%90%D7%A8%D7%93-168-%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%A3-%D7%A0%D7%96%D7%A7-%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8/); [Mako](https://www.mako.co.il/news-business/news/Article-6c23540d40a1d91026.htm); [tazkirim](https://www.tazkirim.gov.il/s/legislativeworkactivity/a13Wn00000BN5wkIAD/%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A6%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%94%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A8?language=en_US)
- Existing: the Tax Authority online system, CPA firms doing it by hand, and calculators (e.g., almo-ai reservist calculator). — [almo-ai](https://almo-ai.com/blog/mahshevon-miluim)
- Gap: an eligibility checker plus calculation plus document assembly plus status tracking across all tracks, for a CPA's whole client base, including a reservist-employer reimbursement tracker for BTL.
- Devil's advocate: demand depends on war events and rules change each time. The government portal is the system of record, there is no global market, and incumbents' CPA software could add calculators. Survives only as a fast cash-generating wedge into CPA offices, not as a venture-scale company.

**4. Insurance-agent commission reconciliation and מסלקה data: SURVIVES (conditional)**
- Pain: 10.9–13.2B NIS a year in commissions across many insurers, with errors such as missing commissions, wrong rates and unmanaged clawbacks. — [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/Insurance/news/article/20045914); [commission.co.il](https://commission.co.il/guides.html)
- Existing: commission.co.il, שווה "פיננסי 360" (מסלקה production pull), and legacy agency systems not researched.
- Gap: possibly an AI-native reconciliation tool for small and mid agencies. Needs validation interviews.
- Devil's advocate: a niche with entrenched vendors, and the regulatory reform (clients paying agents directly) could restructure commission flows. It is purely Israeli with no global extension.

**5. Renovation and small-contractor quote → progress billing → collection: SURVIVES (conditional, needs validation)**
- Pain: contractors get the longest public payment terms (שוטף+70/80), and no found tool combines quotes, change orders, partial bills and receivables. — [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local/articles/0,7340,L-3679788,00.html)
- Existing: Gold Systems (old), ConWize (large builders), Tik-Tok quotes, Mahir, Bizli.
- Devil's advocate: contractors are hard to sell to and low-tech. Global analogs (Buildertrend, Jobber, Houzz Pro) exist, so this is "Israel-first, localized Jobber". The pain evidence is indirect.

**6. Small-employer payroll correctness (payslip audit / Israeli labor-law checker): SURVIVES (weak; old data)**
- Pain: 16.3% payslip error rate at small businesses (2017–18 survey). Payroll services cost 20–60 NIS per slip, audits 800–1,500 NIS, and Israeli rules (overtime, pension base, reservist pay changes from 1.5.2025) are complex. — [Walla](https://finance.walla.co.il/item/3043182); [B144](https://www.b144.co.il/PriceList/%D7%94%D7%A0%D7%94%D7%9C%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA/%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%AA-%D7%AA%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%A9%D7%99-%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%A8/); [BTL](https://www.btl.gov.il/benefits/Reserve_Service/Pages/TagmulZacay.aspx)
- Devil's advocate: Hilan, Michpal and Malam dominate payroll and their pricing was not found. Liability risk is high, the error data is old, and there is no global extension.

### Inferences
- Common thread among survivors: sell to or through **accountants and bookkeepers**. They are trusted, process many SMB clients, carry the regulatory churn and can be reached quickly through Israeli CPA Facebook groups and lishka events. That fits a solo founder testing within weeks.
- Israeli market sizing (rough, inferred): about 600k+ open business files are implied by 61k closures being "the highest in 5 years". Most are solo, so B2B SMB SaaS revenue per customer is 30–150 NIS/month unless sold per-accountant-office. **No authoritative count of עוסקים was retrieved.**
- Global extensibility is best for #2 (collections) and #1 (e-invoice compliance intake as countries adopt clearance models), and weakest for #3, #4 and #6.

### Gaps
- No direct interviews or Facebook-group quotes. Verdicts rely on desk research and vendor pages.
- No API availability check for the Tax Authority allocation-number verification service (critical for #1).
- No count of Israeli insurance agents, CPA offices or bookkeepers was found to size #1, #3 and #4.
- Pricing and complaints for Hilan, Michpal, Synel, Rivhit, Hashavshevet, Priority, Tranzila, Grow/Meshulam, Cardcom, PayBox and Bit were not researched due to tool limits.
