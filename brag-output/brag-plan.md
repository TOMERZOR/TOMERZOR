# SHARD — brag plan

## The brief
"A hype video that showcases the incredible game I made: high energy, music, cool sharp and quick motion graphics, sound effects. Clean, in Apple's design rules."

Run settings: full `/brag`, landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 22 s, music on (HeyGen library, pending network access), SFX on, no voice.

## Planning rubric
1. **What is it?** SHARD (store name "SHARD: Glass Smash"), a physics puzzle for iPhone and Android. You pull back anywhere and release to fire a ball, and glass shatters.
2. **Who is it for?** Phone players who love satisfying destruction and short, clever puzzles: the "oddly satisfying" crowd, casual puzzle fans and Apple-design people.
3. **The one-line pitch:** "Aim. Fire. Shatter." This is the game's own tagline, on its home screen and landing page.
4. **The single most impressive moment:** one shot that chains through a whole level, a ×10 combo of glowing glass, then slow motion on the last piece.
5. **What makes it different:**
   - Every level is proven solvable by a physics solver.
   - The sound: recorded glass layered with crystal bells.
   - A premium, minimal art direction, with five worlds as five times of day.
6. **The real visuals to show:**
   - Live gameplay: Dawn's ×10 chain, Ember's bombs, Night's chandeliers in slow motion.
   - The new level map, with its five worlds as tabs.
   - The 12 balls as the game renders them.
   - The app icon and the SHARD wordmark.
7. **Real copy and claims:**
   - "Aim. Fire. Shatter."
   - "Satisfying physics puzzles" (the App Store subtitle).
   - "Chain reactions."
   - "5 worlds. 120 levels."
   - "Collect 12 balls."
   - "Every level proven solvable."
   - The game isn't released yet, so the closing line is "Coming soon to iPhone & Android."
8. **The brand look:**
   - Type: Nunito 900 for display, 800 for lines.
   - Ink: #3E3A4F.
   - Accent: #FF7A59.
   - Worlds: Dawn #FFF8F1→#F4D6C3, Mint #F2FAF5→#C5E3D3, Lilac #F6F2FC→#D2C6EE, Ember #FFEBD3→#F3AE90, Night #141A2E→#2B3558.
   - Glass tints: #96CDFF, #FFBEC8, #BEAFFF.
   - Icon ground: deep indigo, #2E2A6E→#191339.
9. **Call to action:** the icon plus the wordmark, with "Coming soon to iPhone & Android".

## Angle
**"Every beat breaks."** It's an Apple keynote product film where the music's hits are glass breaking. The type is part of the glass: in the hook, the word "Shatter." physically shatters, and at the end, flying shards assemble back into the SHARD wordmark. Everything in between is the real game, shown big inside a clean phone, on grounds that flood to each world's colour on the beat.

## Tone
This is a freeform direction ("Apple keynote, high energy, sharp"), mapped to `cinematic` pacing with `app-store` cleanliness.
- **Motion:** fast arrivals (0.3–0.5 s, expo.out) that then hold dead still. Cuts land on beats, and a world change lands on a bar line.
- **Grounds:** black for drama, warm white for clarity. Never cluttered.
- **Type:** big declarative sentences ending in periods, one per beat, with one accent word at most.

## Look
- **Canvas:** 1920×1080, 30 fps.
- **Grounds:**
  - Hook: #000.
  - Reveal: Dawn #FFF8F1 with a #F4D6C3 radial glow.
  - Highlights: flood to each world's sky.
  - End: #0E0C1E, which matches the icon's indigo.
- **Display type:** Nunito 900, tracking −0.02 em, 150–240 px for hero words and 96–120 px for headlines. Lines are Nunito 800 at 40–48 px.
- **Phone:** a generic modern phone, 430×932 at a scale of about 0.93 (896 px tall). It has a 14 px graphite rim, a 64 px corner radius and a pill-shaped camera cutout, with a soft shadow of 0 40 80 −20 rgba(0,0,0,.35). The screen shows real 390×844 gameplay captured at 2.5× density.
- **Banned:** dimmed screenshots under titles, every element fading up the same way, floating or breathing loops on landed type, crossfades between worlds, and a corner-docked logo.

## Music cue guidance
- **Target:** high-energy electronic or pop around 120–130 BPM, with a clean, strong drop and bright synths. No vocals, or vocals far back.
- **Placement:** the track's strongest entrance (the drop) on the reveal at 3.75 s. The hook rides the track's build or pre-drop. The end lands on a bar line at about 22.5 s, with a 0.6 s fade under the end card.
- **Timing:** every scene start is written as a beat index. When the real track arrives, `npx hyperframes beats` finds its grid, and the composition is regenerated at its tempo and downbeat.
- **Rules:** the cue metadata is guidance only. Readability and the story come first, and every text line holds at least 0.5 s plus 0.3 s per word.

## SFX plan
- **Sources:** the game's own recorded glass, bombs and cannon (`shard/assets/sfx/`), plus the game's crystal bells taken from each clip's sound log. Whooshes, a riser, an impact and pops come from the HyperFrames bundled library (`CREDITS.md` kept).
- **Placement:** each hit peaks one frame after its picture and sits 3–6 dB over the bed. Repeated whooshes rotate through two or three takes.
