Questions

Is SHARD free?

Yes. The game is free with optional ads. A single "Remove Ads" purchase turns off interstitial ads for good.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything runs on your device. Ads and purchases need a connection, the game does not.

I'm stuck on a level.

Tap the light bulb. A hint shows the first shot of a proven solution. Your first hint in each world is free.

I changed phones. How do I get my purchases back?

Open Settings or the Store and tap "Restore purchases". Remove Ads, the Starter Pack and the Prism ball come back.
