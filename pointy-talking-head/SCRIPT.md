# Pointy — talking-head video script

**Format:** vertical 9:16 Reel / TikTok · about 40–45 seconds · Hebrew · presenter to camera, with screen-recording inserts
**Goal:** a viewer who has never heard of Pointy sends the word "פלאגין" in a DM
**Rules:** written against `CONTENT-PRINCIPLES.md`. The check is at the bottom.

First-person verbs are written for either presenter ("לוחץ/ת" = say "לוחץ" or "לוחצת").

## Three alternative hooks (to A/B test; keep the rest of the script the same)

1. **"על מה לוחצים פה?!"** Said to the laptop in real frustration, then turn to camera. Uses the line that already works in the carousel.
2. **"יש דרך יותר מהירה מיוטיוב, ואף אחד לא מדבר עליה."** Curiosity. Only use it if it's true for your audience.
3. **Silent cold open:** 2 seconds of screen only. The orange cursor glides to a button, click. Then your face: "ראיתם את זה? זה לא אני." It shows before it says (principle 2) and is the strongest for viewers watching muted.

---

## Filming notes

- **Record the demo for real.** One take, at real speed, with a real stopwatch running (principle 2). If it takes 14 seconds, say 14. Never speed up a demo that's presented as real time.
- **Only name apps you recorded.** If you demo in a different program, change "פרמייר" in lines 5 and 7 to that program. Same for the three cuts in line 7.
- **Windows:** "אופשן ורווח" is the Mac shortcut. Line 7 says "במק ובווינדוס" separately on purpose. Don't say the Windows shortcut until you've confirmed what it is.
- **Framing:** 9:16, eye level, window light from the side, the laptop in reach. Leave the top 15% of the frame clear for captions.
- **Captions:** burn them in, in Hebrew. Most Reels are watched muted, and the word "פלאגין" must be readable without sound (principle 6).
- **Delivery:** lines 1–3 tired and dry, line 4 the turn (a small smile), lines 5–6 calm, like showing a friend, line 8 warm and simple. Don't sell; show.
