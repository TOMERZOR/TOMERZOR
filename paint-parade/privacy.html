Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Paint Parade ("the game", "we") is a casual puzzle game. This policy explains what data the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We do not ask for your name, email or account to play.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (level, coins, boosters, gallery, settings) is saved only on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers. Deleting the app deletes this data.

2. Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device model and general location (country/region), and information about how you interact with ads.

  • iOS: we ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised using your IDFA.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) lets you accept or refuse personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore them on your devices; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt.

4. Analytics and crash reports

We may collect anonymous gameplay events (for example "level 12 completed") and crash reports to balance difficulty and fix bugs. These events are not linked to your identity.

6. Children

Paint Parade is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA or Israel's Privacy Protection Law) you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold account data, most requests can be handled by resetting your advertising ID or uninstalling the game; for anything else, email us.
