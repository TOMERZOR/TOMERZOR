# Brag plan: My Old Friend

## Rubric
1. **What is it?** An iPhone app where Moffy, a pink AI helper, looks through the camera (or at the latest screenshot) and tells older people exactly what to press, one step at a time, out loud and in big letters.
2. **Who is it for?** People in their 70s–90s, and the children and grandchildren who get the "how do I…" calls.
3. **The one thing it does better:** it points at the actual button in the picture.
4. **Real product material:** simulator screenshots (pointing at a TV remote, pointing at a Settings screenshot), Moffy's character, the real Yael voice line, the tagline "בינה מלאכותית לגיל השלישי", pricing ₪24.90 / 20 free questions.
5. **Tone:** default (warm, playful, clean), white and pink like the app.
6. **Format:** landscape 1920×1080, Hebrew on screen.
7. **Hook:** the grandparent's messages everyone recognises, stacking up.
8. **Punchline:** Moffy answers instead, and the closing card.
9. **What to avoid:** generic AI language, dark "AI" visuals, unreadable flashes.
