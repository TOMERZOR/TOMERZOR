Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. Our store runs on Shopify, which uses cookies to make the store work and, if you allow it, to understand how the store is used.

Your choices

When you first visit, a cookie banner lets you accept or reject optional cookies. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie Settings link at the bottom of every page. Necessary cookies are always on, because the store can't work without them.

Cookies we use

TypeWhat it doesExamples
NecessaryKeeps your bag, checkout, login and security working, and remembers your cookie choice.cart, _shopify_essential, _tracking_consent, localization
AnalyticsShows us how visitors use the store so we can improve it. Only with your consent._shopify_y, _shopify_s, _shopify_sa_p
MarketingMeasures our ads and shows relevant ads on other sites. Only with your consent, and only if we use advertising tools.Set by ad platforms we connect, for example Meta or TikTok

Shopify keeps a full, up-to-date list of the cookies it sets in its cookie policy.

Browser storage

Our theme also saves your wishlist in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. If you do, your bag will be emptied and you may need to sign in again.
